# Demo script
1. Reset the demo and choose the Colorado Buffaloes, then Travis Hunter.
2. Call out the black-and-gold mountain stage and the personalized Saturday cards.
3. Move through Buffs–Nebraska, the Big 12 outlook, and Hunter’s player story.
4. Open the Buffs Quiz. Complete the three stories and questions to reveal the rare Rashaan Salaam card.
5. Search for `Travis Hunter` and open the result card.
6. Move through the four-image two-way archive, then take the Hunter quiz and claim the legendary card.
7. Search `Compare Travis Hunter and Ashton Jeanty` to show a grounded, on-premise comparison.
8. Use the reset control to clear cards and avatar state for the next demo.
